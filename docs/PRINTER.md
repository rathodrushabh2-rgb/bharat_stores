# Printer at the shop

## How printing works
1. The owner makes a bill on his phone from anywhere.
2. The shop phone (logged in as staff, new-order alerts turned on) gets a notification:
   *नया ऑर्डर #130 · …*. Tapping it opens the bill.
3. Staff tap **प्रिंट** → **Print / प्रिंट** → choose the printer → **Print**.
4. Staff pack, tap **पैक हो गया**, deliver, tap **दे दिया**.

The bill is always stored in the app's database. The paper is the customer's copy and the shop's
signed backup for udhaar.

## Recommended: WiFi mono laser printer (no thermal, no fading)
**Brother HL-L2351DW**: mono laser, WiFi + USB, automatic two-sided, A4/A5.
- Laser toner is fused into the paper, so the print does not fade with heat or light the way thermal
  paper does. It is the right choice for udhaar bills kept for years.
- Prints Hindi exactly as shown on screen, because the phone renders the page.
- Prints from an Android phone over WiFi with no computer: Android's built-in print service
  (Mopria / Brother Print Service plugin from the Play Store).
- Indian prices seen online: about ₹10,500–14,200. Toner TN-2365 lasts about 2,600 pages
  (original about ₹2,300). Check with the seller that it is the right toner for this model.

In the app, choose print size **A4 · 2 कॉपी** once (the phone remembers it): one A4 sheet gives a
**ग्राहक प्रति** (customer copy) and a **दुकान प्रति** (shop copy) with a cut line. Udhaar bills get a
**ग्राहक के हस्ताक्षर** (signature) line. File the signed shop copies.

### What the shop needs
- The printer, A4 paper, and a WiFi network: any home WiFi router, or a spare phone's hotspot.
- One Android phone or tablet at the shop that stays logged in (staff login).
- One-time setup: connect the printer to the WiFi (WPS button on the router or the Brother app),
  then on the phone go to Settings → Connected devices → Printing → turn on "Default print service" /
  install "Brother Print Service Plugin", and print a test page.

## Alternatives considered
- **Dot-matrix with carbon copies (TVS MSP 240 Star, Epson TM-U220)**: ribbon prints also last, and
  give carbon copies, but they need a Windows PC (Android cannot drive them). 9-pin heads print Hindi
  only as rough graphics. Not recommended for this app.
- **Ink-tank inkjet**: works over WiFi, but many inks are dye-based and fade, and heads dry out when
  printing is light. Laser is more reliable for records.
- **Thermal receipt printers**: fade within months. Avoid for udhaar.
